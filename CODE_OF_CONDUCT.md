# Code of Conduct

PyQuest is a learning project, and many people who open issues here are beginners. Contributors and maintainers commit to making participation a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient and welcoming, especially with people who are new to programming.
- Give feedback about code and content, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Accept constructive criticism and credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.

## Scope

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@ali-kin4](https://github.com/ali-kin4), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
